# Nexolume (site)

## Register

brand

## Users

Windows users who want to write, calculate and present on their own computer, offline, without creating an account. They come to the site to find out whether the alpha delivers what it promises and to download the archive. Two languages: French (primary) and English.

## Product Purpose

Present Nexolume, a native office suite made of Writer, Sheets and Slides, and get people to download the 0.1 alpha. The site says exactly what works and what is missing. Success: a visitor understands in under a minute what the alpha does and can install it.

## Brand Personality

Clear, precise, personal. The document comes before the packaging. An honest craftsperson's tone: no schedule promises, no invented numbers.

## Anti-references

No generic SaaS landing page (gradients, fake metrics, grid of identical cards, "10x faster"). No copy of the Microsoft Word page. No neon or fluorescent green. No decorative glass effect. No reflexively numbered sections.

## Design Principles

- Three spaces, three full colour fields: Writer blue, Sheets sage, Slides amber.
- The app picker is the live element: choosing Writer, Sheets or Slides recolours the whole first screen.
- Real state before promise: the alpha's limits have their own section.
- Every claim about the product matches a function verified by the application's tests.
- French comes first; English is a real translation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA minimum: verified contrast (body text ≥ 4.5:1), visible keyboard focus, explicit labels, skip link to the content. Reduced motion respected (`prefers-reduced-motion`). Smooth reading on phones and 1080p screens.
